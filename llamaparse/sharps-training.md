# Sharps Identification

* Sharps come in many forms, from discarded needles to broken glass or crockery and Stanley knives

* Sharps are very often left in areas that may not be obvious, they can often be concealed behind handrails, toilets or even stored in bin bags

* When dealing in such areas you should always operate with caution and work as though a sharp maybe present

* Sharps can lead to infections entering the body via the wounds they create, infections such as Hep C can lead to very serious conditions

* Never put your hands into a location that you cannot see is safe

---



# Reducing the risk

**NEVER** put hands into/onto 'blind' obstructed areas without visually checking the area for sharps.

**NEVER** put hands into waste bins to remove any items, remove the bin liner completely in one go and transfer into a secondary waste sack.

# Disposal

UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES ARE YOU TO REMOVE A NEEDLE OR A SHARP WITH YOUR FINGERS!

* Needles/Sharps must only be removed using the correct piece of equipment such as a pair of tongs/tweezers or a litter picker

* This puts distance between the needle and the person removing it from the location which reduces the likelihood of any form of contact.

* Needles that have been found and collected safely must then be placed into a yellow clinical disposal unit

* If you are unsure at any point in regards to sharps removal contact your line manager immediately

---



# Needle/Sharps Injury

If you do come into contact with a needle or a sharp you must follow the below guidelines.

* You must **MUST NOT** put injured body part in your mouth and suck on the wound.

* You should encourage the wound to bleed – so that infections such as Hepatitis C do not enter the body.

* Run injury under running water in an attempt to flush out any potential harmful bacteria.

* Seek immediate assistance from your supervisor or line manager and report to Health and Safety

* Take the needle in a rigid sided container, see example, and take to the hospital accompanied by a colleague.
