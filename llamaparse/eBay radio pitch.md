# Key considerations when developing sonic branding

## Do

* Create something ownable

* Tie audio to core messaging

* Consider all usage permutations across the entire enterprise

* Understand the consumer experience / emotional connection

* Think long-term

* Be consistent

## Don't

* Create multiple opposing sonic IDs

* Attempt to emulate an existing brand's sonic branding

* Pastiche an established tune / song

* Force fit audio where none exists

* Expect iconic status overnight

* Be afraid to tweak elements over time to stay relevant

# Voice

We recommend using a consistent voice artist in all spots, that becomes synonymous as the 'eBay voice' on radio (and beyond) in each market.

As such, the voice in each market should have a personality that embodies the brand's optimism, vibrancy, straightforwardness, wit and conversational manner.

A single voice leaves little room for inconsistencies across markets.

# Tone

The tone of voice of the campaign should be consistent with our wider brand tone of: **Spirited, Dependable, Real, Smart**.

We will deliver all messaging very clearly, in a friendly and straightforward manner, that is also witty and clever, to engage our audience.

---



# Narrative

The narrative style and structure of our radio comms should be **consistent** across the campaign.

We're not here to create intrigue.

We need to establish that this is an eBay commercial as soon as possible in the spot.

Music, voiced call-outs of the eBay name, and owned **sound cues** can all be used to communicate our brand in the first few seconds.

---



Each commercial will have a consistent script structure to deliver all key messaging, TOV and branding throughout the spot, that is **simple to replicate** in different languages.

And can accommodate multiple offers and messaging.

---



# ▶ Music

We should always seek to use music consistently across our audio and visual comms.

While we are using the 'Montage' track in our TV campaign, we should also use the track in our radio comms.

---



# Sound

Any consistent sound cues – or mnemonic devices – that products can use throughout their user touch points are powerful branding tools.

We should, therefore, seek to employ any owned sonic device across all our audio and visual comms.

# Why it works

It's instantly recognisable and eBay to the core – taken from an existing asset, not re-imagined.

The trill effect evokes colour. When we hear the sound, a rainbow spectrum of colour is painted in our mind's eye.

Amazon don't have a notification sound, so this is something we can own.

What it represents is universal – triggering the same feeling the world over.

---



🔊🔊🔊🔊🔊🔊🔊

As we are talking to customers who have used eBay in the past, they will recognise the sound and give them that winning feeling, but if not, they certainly will recognise it after hearing the radio ad when they start using eBay again.

# Concept 1.

## Why fill your cart with colour?

---



# Concept 1
# System

The simplest, most consistent way to follow our TV comms, is to replicate it aurally.

Each TV spot begins with a question about the boring retail status quo. Then quickly introduces the colourful eBay world as the modern, vibrant antidote, with a fun product story,

leading to a consistent CTA to Fill Your Cart With Colour.

So, in the same way, each radio spot starts by posing a question. Then quickly bring in our eBay Trrrinnnggg! mnemonic to sonically herald in our colourful eBay world. The TV music will also feature throughout. And we will call out the eBay brand in

the first few seconds of each spot.

Each commercial will be written within the following framework to maximise brand consistency across the campaign, regardless of the individual messaging or the market it appears in.

# Concept 2 System

How do we create different interest in each spot, without it being a production nightmare when we re-record for different markets? We use sound effects, that only need to be created once at the master recording stage.

Everyone's cart is coloured differently. We will celebrate this with a sound effect campaign.

Each spot starts with our eBay Trrrinnnggg! mnemonic to sonically establish the brand. Quickly followed by a voiceover line asking 'what's in your eBay cart?' We will then hear an engaging sound effect, whilst our narrator offers three explanations of the sound - two of them genuine eBay items, and one ridiculous.

As we reach the CTA, our eBay music kicks in, as our narrator delivers the line: Fill Your Cart With Colour Each commercial will be written within the following framework to maximise brand consistency across the campaign, regardless of the individual messaging or the market it appears in.

# Concept 3
## System

The eBay Trrrinnnggg! mnemonic alert sound has a such a strong positive affect on our users, let's make it the star of our audio comms.

The little 'tingle' of excitement that our users describe when the alert goes off on their phone, is a hugely powerful asset.

If we celebrate the unique and positive 'eBay feeling' that you can only get by using our brand, then we will attract new users and stimulate use from our current audience.

In each ad, we open on the Trrrinnnggg! mnemonic. Followed by our narrator eulogising the 'eBay tingle' of using our product. Then, as the eBay music kicks in, the narrator explains how the product offering in this spot is a great way to give listeners the 'eBay tingle'. Followed by our brand line of 'Fill Your Cart With Colour'.

## Why it works

### [x] Integrated with the 'Fill your cart with colour' platform

Consistent TOV. This campaign heroes the eBay mnemonic device, which paints colour for us aurally. The current brand music and CTA are also used.

### [x] Consistent in structure and flexes across executions

Each script follows a tight structure, The structure is flexible enough to carry different messaging in a charming and engaging manner. And uses consistently branded music and sonic device.

### [x] Considered when it comes to production complexities

The mnemonic device provokes a human reaction which is true on all markets. A single voice is used, that can be casted once in each market, and used across the whole campaign. Music and branding device can be used across all markets. Each script is written to accommodate dynamic radio options.

# The benefits of DAX radio

## Targeting

Working alongside MediaCom, we will use precise targeting based on segmentation. We can be forensic in making sure our messaging goes to exactly the right audience.

**For example – A Home and Garden promotion skewed to 30+. Whereas fashion content about shoes targeted to females 24-34.**

## Dynamic

With dynamic targeting, we can be hyper-personal and contextually relevant.

**Radio System is designed to accommodate multiple-messaging within each script. So, we can make each spot bespoke to the listener, depending on variables such as time, date, weather, location and even name.**

## Sequential

Once we've engaged with our audience, we can serve them with follow-up content, to create a longer, personalised conversation that prompts a stronger call to action.

**Our radio system is designed to accommodate follow-up messaging to keep the conversation going and push the listener harder towards action. (With bags of charm, of course.)**
