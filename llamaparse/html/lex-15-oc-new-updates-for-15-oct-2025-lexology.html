New Updates for 15-Oct-2025

Blog OverRuled - Sanctions & Export Controls Updates

Akin Gump Strauss Hauer & Feld LLP

China, United Kingdom, USA October 15 2025

The OverRuled: Russia Trade Controls Resource Center has been updated with the following actions:

  • 13 Oct 2025 (UK): The Office of Trade Sanctions Implementation ("OTSI") issued a blog post to demonstrate how good due diligence practices in a case involving financial services can prevent breaches of UK trade sanctions measures, such as those imposed by the Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (the "Russia Regulations").

The OverRuled: China Trade Controls Resource Center has been updated with the following actions:

  • 14 Oct 2025 (China - Measures and Countermeasures): The Ministry of Commerce announced sanctions against five U.S. subsidiaries of Hanwha Ocean, the South Korean shipbuilder formerly known as Daewoo Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering, for assisting and supporting the U.S. government in conducting the Section 301 investigation and imposing measures against China's maritime, logistics, and shipbuilding industries.
