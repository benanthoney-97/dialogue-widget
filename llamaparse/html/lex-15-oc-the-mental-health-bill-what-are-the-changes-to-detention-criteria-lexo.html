The Mental Health Bill - What are the changes to detention criteria?

Hill Dickinson

United Kingdom October 15 2025

Click here to watch the video

The Mental Health Bill is making its way through Parliament with 3rd reading in the House of Commons anticipated in or around October 2025 before it reaches its final stage dealing with any amendments before gaining Royal Assent. Here, Louise Wilson and Rachael Watkinson from our Healthcare and Public Law team discuss the planned changes to detention criteria, the impact of changes for those with learning disability and/or autism, how this will impact providers and commissioners and some tips for organisations in planning ahead.
