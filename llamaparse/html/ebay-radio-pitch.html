Strategic Approach

Sonic Branding is the approach of using unique, proprietary sound and music to convey a brand's essence and values.

Xbox use a mnemonic across platforms to strengthen their sonic branding

When you turn on your Xbox

FEEL TRUE POWER

At the end of Xbox TV ads

At the end of Xbox radio ads

Many other big brands are doing it well

Coca-Cola
Five-note melody integrated into pop songs featuring credible artists		 Mcdonalds
Sonic logo started life as the musical output of an artist partnership		 Intel
Created an auditory signature to bring sensory awareness to unseen product

Sonic branding helps to...

  • Instantly confirm the identity of the brand

  • Improve brand recall

  • Trigger existing emotional connection to the brand

Key considerations when developing sonic branding

Do

  • Create something ownable

  • Tie audio to core messaging

  • Consider all usage permutations across the entire enterprise

  • Understand the consumer experience / emotional connection

  • Think long-term

  • Be consistent

Don't

  • Create multiple opposing sonic IDs

  • Attempt to emulate an existing brand's sonic branding

  • Pastiche an established tune / song

  • Force fit audio where none exists

  • Expect iconic status overnight

  • Be afraid to tweak elements over time to stay relevant

Variety of touchpoints to use sonic branding

Marketing

  • TV / radio ads
  • Customer Service

Functionality

  • On website, in app
  • Voice interfaces

Corporate

  • Trade marketing
  • B2B comms
  • Internal comms

So we laid down some ground rules

Voice

We recommend using a consistent voice artist in all spots, that becomes synonymous as the 'eBay voice' on radio (and beyond) in each market.

As such, the voice in each market should have a personality that embodies the brand's optimism, vibrancy, straightforwardness, wit and conversational manner.

A single voice leaves little room for inconsistencies across markets.

Tone

The tone of voice of the campaign should be consistent with our wider brand tone of: Spirited, Dependable, Real, Smart.

We will deliver all messaging very clearly, in a friendly and straightforward manner, that is also witty and clever, to engage our audience.

Narrative

The narrative style and structure of our radio comms should be consistent across the campaign.

We're not here to create intrigue.

We need to establish that this is an eBay commercial as soon as possible in the spot.

Music, voiced call-outs of the eBay name, and owned sound cues can all be used to communicate our brand in the first few seconds.

Each commercial will have a consistent script structure to deliver all key messaging, TOV and branding throughout the spot, that is simple to replicate in different languages.

And can accommodate multiple offers and messaging.

▶ Music

We should always seek to use music consistently across our audio and visual comms.

While we are using the 'Montage' track in our TV campaign, we should also use the track in our radio comms.

Sound

Any consistent sound cues – or mnemonic devices – that products can use throughout their user touch points are powerful branding tools.

We should, therefore, seek to employ any owned sonic device across all our audio and visual comms.

DAX Radio

dax

DIGITAL AUDIO EXCHANGE

The benefits of DAX radio

Targeting

Working alongside MediaCom, we will use precise targeting based on segmentation. We can be forensic in making sure our messaging goes to exactly the right audience.

For example – A Home and Garden promotion skewed to 30+. Whereas fashion content about shoes targeted to females 24-34.

Dynamic

With dynamic targeting, we can be hyper-personal and contextually relevant.

Radio System is designed to accommodate multiple-messaging within each script. So, we can make each spot bespoke to the listener, depending on variables such as time, date, weather, location and even name.

Sequential

Once we've engaged with our audience, we can serve them with follow-up content, to create a longer, personalised conversation that prompts a stronger call to action.

Our radio system is designed to accommodate follow-up messaging to keep the conversation going and push the listener harder towards action. (With bags of charm, of course.)
