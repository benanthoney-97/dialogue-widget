# Equipment Safety Check

* When selecting the appropriate piece of equipment you must ensure it is safe to use

* Ensure you are familiar with the manufacturers operating instructions and you are able to refer to them whenever necessary (stored in training app)

* Check to ensure that the equipment has a portable appliance testing (PAT) label and that the test has been completed within the specified time period, each piece of equipment must be tested every year


---



# Health and Safety Compliance

* To obtain your licence to practice with Wetton you must complete the mandatory training modules and any relevant cleaning task modules. Until such time your work will be monitored more closely.

* You must have read the following documentation before completing each task you undertake, all of the documents are stored in the training app

* RAMS – (Risk Assessment and Method Statement) Documents that show you the safest and quickest way to do the job

* COSHH assessments – Risk assessments for chemicals, provides instructions on how to use every chemical we use safely

---



# Health and Safety Considerations

* On arrival at the workplace make sure you are looking for any potential hazards that may cause you harm, such as slip and trip hazards

* Identify the relevant COSHH assessment for any chemicals you are going to use, make sure you are fully aware of this document before starting work

* Identify the RAMS required for the task you will be completing, make yourself fully aware of the contents of this document

* You will find both of these documents within the training app

---



* Wash hands before starting work activities in line with the company policy, make you follow the approved method as detailed in posters above your washing station

* Ensure you have the correct PPE on, as stated by the COSHH and RAMS documentation

* Before starting work make sure you have placed relevant warning signs so that other people know you will be working in this area and that you may have created a hazard, eg wet floor


---



# Accident Procedures

* If you have an accident at any time while at work you must make your line manager aware whenever you are able too or it is safe to do so

* Each workplace will have a first aid box and a first aid appointed person this may be your manager or supervisor, you should make yourself aware of this person before you start working


---



# Site Evacuation Procedures

* The fire and site evacuation procedures will depend on your location at which you work.

* A site induction will provide this information but there also ensure you read and be familiar with your site's evacuation procedure

* Make yourself aware with the nearest evacuation assembly points

* DO NOT COMPLETE ANY TASK YOU HAVE NOT HAD TRAINING. IF UNSURE ON ANYTHING AT ALL CONTACT YOUR LINE MANAGER
