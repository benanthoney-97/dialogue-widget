

# SRM potential benefits and risks

Sunlight reflection methods (SRM) could lower global temperatures, reducing many risks, but introducing others. Some examples:

<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Category</th>
<th>POTENTIAL SRM BENEFITS</th>
<th>POTENTIAL SRM RISKS</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>FOOD</strong></td>
<td>By reducing warming, SRM might boost crop yields overall</td>
<td>Changes in rainfall due to SRM could reduce crop yields in some regions.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>WATER</strong></td>
<td>SRM would change rainfall patterns, improving water security in some regions</td>
<td>Those changed rainfall patterns might cause worsening water security in other regions.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>HEALTH</strong></td>
<td>SRM would reduce extreme heat and its health impacts, reduce shifts in the spread of diseases overall</td>
<td>SRM could delay the recovery of the ozone hole and add to air pollution, posing health risks.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>ECOSYSTEMS</strong></td>
<td>Lower temperatures would reduce coral bleaching. Reduced climate change would mean fewer extinctions overall, but potentially more extinctions in some regions.</td>
<td>SRM would not address ocean acidification which would still threaten coral reefs. Reduced climate change could potentially mean more extinctions in some regions.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>EXTREME WEATHER</strong></td>
<td>Many weather extremes would be less intense, including reduced extreme rainfall overall.</td>
<td>Droughts may be more intense in some regions with SRM.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>ECONOMY</strong></td>
<td>Reduced climate change could mean reduced economic impacts with SRM.</td>
<td>Reduced water availability in some regions could have economic consequences.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>SECURITY</strong></td>
<td>Less intense climate extremes could reduce the risk of violent conflict.</td>
<td>There could be conflict over the use of SRM.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

Source: SRM360.org
