## RAMS & Staff Training – Safety Spotlight - June

Under the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974 WCSL has a duty of care to all employees (both temporary and permanent) through the provision of <ins>INFORMATION, INSTRUCTION, TRAINING AND SUPERVISION</ins>

Under the same act, <ins>YOU</ins>, the employee, have a responsibility to act in a safe manor and co-operate with WCSL for everyone's <ins>SAFETY</ins>

<ins>BOTH YOU AND WETTON CAN BE PROSECUTED FOR FAILURES TO PROVIDE A SAFE WORKING ENVIRONMENT</ins>

So as a general employee you should have had:

1) An induction to your regular place(s) of work. An induction is either a brief talk, a paper or electronic set of information (or both), to have included the following information;

* Site layout (including where you can and cannot go)
* Emergency procedures (including Fire Assembly Point and Evacuation Routes)
* Site Rules (smoking etc)
* First aid provision (where they are, who they are)
* Welfare provisions (where you can eat your lunch, or go to the toilet)

<ins>REMEMBER</ins> each site is <ins>DIFFERENT</ins> and each may have different procedures and rules!

2) Training in all relevant modules on the training app which are linked to the RAMS.

3) Any additional Training as required by your Job Role which may involve additional RAMS.

Key points to look out for when reading a risk assessment –
* The task specific hazard – this will give you a clear indication of what hazards are present when completing this task
* Control measure – make yourself familiar with the control measures as these are designed to keep you safe
* Risk rating – you will notice that each task has a risk rating before controls and after controls, this give you an indication of how much the controls are there to help keep you safe
* The procedure – the procedure will provide you with a detailed description of how the job should be completed safely and effectively, if you feel this can be improved please inform your line manager

**YOU SHOULD NOT** start a task you are asked to do without understanding the risks to you or anyone else around you. If you are unsure of anything you must speak up and tell someone. If you feel pressured into signing your Training Record then raise a training issue with your manager or supervisor.

**DO NOT** sign or complete your training module if you are unsure or have not understood the training provided. If you are having trouble understanding the training in the APP or you do not understand the training or briefing then contact your manger or Wetton H&S manger immediately. You will not get into trouble, it will allow us to ensure you are trained properly.

You **MUST** be able to **DEMONSTRATE** upon request how to safely undertake a task. This also forms part of your **ONGOING** training!

IF you are concerned about your level of knowledge speak directly to your Line Manager or Health and Safety on 0207 237 2007 or at healthandsafety@wettons.co.uk

<ins>A SIGNATURE WILL NOT ENSURE YOUR SAFETY. UNDERSTANDING IT WILL</ins>

<ins>MAKE SURE YOU FULLY UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU ARE BEING ASKED TO DO BEFORE YOU DO IT</ins>

As a **Supervisor**:

You should already have experience with different tasks (although maybe not all) and you may have experience of different sites, or be asked to supervise different tasks.

**AGAIN**, you shouldn't undertake anything without understanding the risks to you or anyone around you.

You shouldn't ask anyone to go anywhere, or perform a task they are not trained for, or are capable of and you should be prepared to **DEMONSTRATE** your knowledge and training too

You should be also regularly assessing the capabilities and training requirements of your staff

IF you are concerned about your level of knowledge speak directly to your line manager or Health and Safety at healthandsafety@wettons.co.uk
