## Definition:
"The cleaning of any floor surface within the workplace or designated area using a dry buffer machine".

## Legal Duties:
**The Health & Safety at Work Act 1974 (HASAWA)**, which places an obligation on the employer to provide a safe place of work.

**The Management of Health & Safety at Work Regulations**, compel employers to carry out risk assessments that are suitable and sufficient and require: to assess the risks to health from the given task and decide what controls are needed; use those controls and make sure workers use them; make sure the controls are working properly and to inform and train workers about the risks to their health.

**Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations** require that risks to people's health and safety, from machinery that they use at work, be prevented or controlled

## Types of Hazards:
Electricity; Slips Trips & Falls; Manual Handling; COSHH.

## Control Measures:
* Assemble and pre-use check the required equipment for safety and place warning signs so as to be visible to pedestrians from all access routes
* If the polishing machine is fitted with a tank, disconnect the water control before spray cleaning.
* In a correctly labelled spray bottle, prepare a cleaning solution, dilute in accordance with the chemical manufacturer's instructions.
* **Only an approved company product is to be used.**
* **Ensure that both a relevant COSHH Assessment and Safety Data Sheet are available and are read prior to product use and signed off.**
* Spray a fine mist over an area of about 2 metres, taking care not to over spray other surfaces.
* Using overlapping passes, pass the machine over the damp area and buff until dry.
* Clean the area adjacent to skirting by 'cutting in' parallel to skirting. Wipe skirting with cloth if necessary.
* Position the cable behind the line of work to ensure it is not caught up by the pad/brush, or tripped over. Check, turn over, change or clean pad/brush frequently.
* Sweep/mop the floor after buffing, if applicable to do so. Clean all equipment and return to the designated storage area

**NOTE 1: DO NOT SPRAY OVER A WIDE AREA ONLY SPRAY IN THE IMMEDIATE AREA ENSURE WARNING SIGNAGE IS CLEARLY VISIBLE**

**NOTE 2: UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES WHEN USING THE BUFFING MACHINE IS THE OPERATIVE TO PLACE THE CABLE OVER THEIR SHOULDER AT ANY TIME!**

* Report any 'Malfunctions, Damage or Vandalism' to your line manager

## Prior to the Activity:
* Ensure you have read the respective task Risk Assessments. If they are not available report to your supervisor immediately and seek further advice.

## Supervision:
* Ensure that employees are trained, informed and supervised in safety awareness.
* Conduct regular self-inspections of your facility (inside and out) to identify faults and/or hazards and eliminate them
* Investigate all accidents, even those in which no injury occurs, and take steps to prevent recurrences
* Ensure workplace inspections/audits are carried out i.e. housekeeping, storage, office spaces, electrical registers, fire fighting equipment and replenishments
